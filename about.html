About Me

Heyo, I’m Lisa!

I’m a freshman at Stanford University studying Math and Computer Science, hoping to minor in Design. I love storytelling through visual arts, from relatable comics to murals advocating for women in the literary field.

My works interweave my interest in social activism, such as Asian-American rights and political philosophy. But I also love paying homage to my favorite music artists (Olivia Rodrigo/Fleetwood Mac/Audrey Hobert) through fanart!

I also love writing (you can find some on my Substack), finding new routes for running, and taking pride in my exhaustive log of Starbucks orders, ranked according to a secret and extremely accurate metric.
