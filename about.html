About Me

Hi, I'm Lisa — an artist working across paint, wall, and page. I grew up filling the margins of notebooks, and never really stopped; my studio now is just a bigger margin.

I care about work that feels warm before it feels clever — pieces people want to sit with, not just look at. Lately I've been drawn to soft color fields and the space where illustration turns into pattern.

When I'm not painting, I'm probably reorganizing my sketchbook shelf or trying a new Starbucks order — I keep an ongoing log of everything I've tried, ranked with complete seriousness.
